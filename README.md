# Privacy Policy for CleverioJot

Effective date: November 11, 2026

CleverioJot ("the app"), made by Alargee, is a private journal with an on-device AI rant partner and daily insights. This policy explains what the app accesses and how your information is handled.

## 1. Information the App Handles

When you use CleverioJot, the app stores the following on your device:

- **Journal entries** you write in Your Thoughts.
- **Chats** with your rant partner in Your Feelings.
- **Daily summaries and moods** created from your chats each night, and short facts the rant partner remembers about you.
- **App settings**, such as your chosen rant partner character.

## 2. On-Device Processing

- All AI features (chat replies, daily summaries, Daily Insights and writing correction) run **on your device** using an on-device language model.
- Your journal entries, chats, summaries and moods are **never sent to our servers or to any third-party AI service**.
- We do not have servers that receive your data. We do not collect, analyze, sell or share any personal information.

## 3. Internet Access

The app uses an internet connection only to download its on-device AI model through Google Play after you install the app. No journal or chat content is sent during this download. After the model is downloaded, the app works offline.

## 4. Data Storage, Backup and Deletion

- All data is stored locally on your device.
- If you have Android device backup turned on, your device may include the app's data in your personal Google account backup. This backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us.
- You can delete all of your data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## 5. Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Sends your Daily Insight in the morning, an evening reminder, and a notice when your rant partner has replied while you were away. You can turn notifications off in your device settings. |
| Internet / Network state | Downloads the on-device AI model through Google Play. |

## 6. Not a Medical Service

CleverioJot is a journaling and self-reflection tool. It does not provide medical, psychological or therapeutic advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

## 7. Children's Privacy

CleverioJot is intended for users aged 18 and over. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date at the top.
